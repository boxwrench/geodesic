## Land ownership / fragmentation — Union Pacific checkerboard ✅
- 1862/1864 Pacific Railroad Acts granted UP odd-numbered sections (later 20 sections/mi) → **checkerboard** of alternating private/BLM 1-mi sections for ~20 mi each side of the rail line across Laramie, Albany, Carbon, **Sweetwater**, Uinta counties (~4.58M ac in WY Territory).
- WY ~46.7% federally owned (29.1M / 62.3M ac); BLM manages ~18.4M surface ac + 42.9M ac mineral estate statewide. Sweetwater is among the most federal/checkerboard-heavy.
- Today: non-BLM checkerboard sections mostly ag or oil/gas. Large blocks (ex-Occidental) have been offered for sale (Cowboy State Daily 2023; SweetwaterNOW — state withdrew a bid). → **Path-0 / override angle for assembly.**
- Implication: contiguous private ≥5,000-ac blocks are hard inside the checkerboard; assembly is the central feasibility question. Sweetwater publishes a detailed online ownership/checkerboard map (use at AOI).
- Sources: WyoHistory.org "Industry, Politics and Power: the Union Pacific in Wyoming"; Wyo4News "The Union Pacific checkerboard"; BLM Wyoming; Stacker/Ballotpedia (federal %); Cowboy State Daily 2023-07-11; SweetwaterNOW.

## Transmission ✅ (attractor)
- **Jim Bridger Substation** (Point of Rocks, near Rock Springs): 230 kV + 500 kV; terminus of Gateway West Transmission Line Phase 1 (160 mi from Shirley Basin). Adjacent to Jim Bridger Power Plant.
- → well inside the placeholder spec's ~10-15 mi max-distance-to-≥115kV threshold for SW-county sites; power capacity near an industrial load is favorable.
- Sources: Wyoming News "BLM approves segments of Gateway West"; project descriptions.

## Federal layer — greater sage-grouse ✅ (primary handle)
- Sweetwater contains designated WY **Sage-Grouse Core Areas** (e.g., Salt Wells; "Power" core area referenced bordering the Jackalope Wind project area). Core-Area policy constrains surface disturbance/siting; ESA petition history for the species makes it the live federal/NEPA hook here.
- §404/wetlands exposure expected LOW (arid, <10" precip basin) — favorable per framework/07.
- Sources: Gillette News Record / Wyoming News (Jackalope Wind, Salt Wells & Power core areas); framework/07 logic.
