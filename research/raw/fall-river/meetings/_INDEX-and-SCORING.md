## STAGE 1 — Discover (host = capacity signal)
- 🔍 **Host:** self-hosted WordPress site, minutes/agendas/packets as PDFs at `fallrivercountysd.gov/files/YYYY/MM/...` (mirror of legacy `fallriver.sdcounties.org`; site changed primary address 2026-05-14). **No Granicus/Legistar/YouTube/video.** PDF-only clerk site = a low-to-mid capacity tier (lower than Sweetwater's Revize+Granicus stack), BUT the archive is unusually complete and well-indexed (every meeting 2024–2026 has Minutes + Agenda + Packet links). Fetchable in plain text via web_fetch. No records request needed.
- 🔍 **NO ZONING is the headline Stage-1 finding.** County P&Z page states verbatim: *"Outside of Edgemont and Hot Springs **there is no planning and zoning.**"* The only county land-use touchpoints are: (a) **plats** of subdivisions (recorded under Ord. 2018-01 & 2012-02, SDCL 11-3 framework), (b) **plats adjacent to a city boundary** require joint county+city approval (SDCL 11-3-6), (c) **approach permits** (driveway access to county roads), (d) **ROW-occupancy permits** (utilities in county road ROW), (e) **floodplain building permits** (only inside FEMA floodplain), (f) **road-district incorporations**. Septic = state (DANR, SDCL 74:53:01:16, 1-acre min lot w/ private well). The county explicitly off-loads building permits to the cities and to the State (plumbing/electrical).
- 🔍 Confirmed by Feb-19-2026 minutes: even **airport-adjacent zoning** is the City of Hot Springs' job — *"The City of Hot Springs will need zoning control in order to receive grants."* The county does not zone.
- Population ~7,453 (rural). Cadence: Commission meets **1st & 3rd Thursdays** (~2×/month) + occasional special/equalization meetings.

## Comparison to Sweetwater (84.5)
🔍 **Higher: 88.5 vs 84.5 (+4).** BUT the +4 is driven by *structural absence of regulation*, not by an observed pro-growth machine. Fall River scores higher on approval ratio (no denials, no discretionary refusals possible), contention (literally no contested land-use docket), and throughput (no P&Z→Board lag). It scores LOWER only on scale-ceiling proximity, where the record is thin.

**The grades are not apples-to-apples and the comparison is fragile:**
- Sweetwater's 84.5 reflects a *real, active* P&Z board that approves applications at ~86–96% — a measured friendliness of an institution that *does* gate development.
- Fall River's 88.5 reflects the *absence* of a gate at all. At the county level there is no zoning to be denied by. That is maximally "friendly" to as-of-right rural development, but it also means the county is **not the binding constraint** and the meeting record carries little signal about how a *large/novel* project (utility-scale solar/wind, CAFO, industrial) would actually fare — because the county neither permits nor reviews such uses (they route to the city, to state DANR/PUC siting, or are by-right). The 88.5 should be read as "low-capacity, low-friction, low-information," NOT as "actively pro-development board."

## Candidate friendliness markers observed (for the marker loop)
1. 🔍 **"No county zoning" as a structural friendliness ceiling-AND-floor** — by-right rural land use; the constraint moves to (a) state septic/floodplain, (b) the *city* for boundary-adjacent/airport land, (c) state PUC/DANR for large energy/livestock. Marker: *when a county disclaims P&Z, the meeting-grade saturates high but loses predictive power for large projects.*
2. 🔍 **"Contingent on taxes being current"** as the only recurring plat condition — a fiscal gate, not a land-use gate. (Recurs across 2024–2026; candidate for the keyword list as a *non-friction* token.)
3. 🔍 **Pro-property-rights / anti-federal-mandate board resolutions** (EID tags, Argentine beef, BNSF terminal) — a political-culture tell that predicts permissiveness toward private land use.
4. 🔍 **Road-district incorporation by landowner petition** (Sheps Canyon Estates) — the rural substitute for HOA/subdivision infrastructure governance; appears where there is no zoning.
