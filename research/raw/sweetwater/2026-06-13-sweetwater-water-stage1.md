## Great Divide Basin (closed/endorheic)
- ~4,000 sq mi, primarily Sweetwater + Carbon counties; Continental Divide splits and encloses it → endorheic, **no surface outlet**. Precip <10"/yr; Wyoming Basin shrub steppe.
- Implication: a candidate site inside the Great Divide is a Stage 1 water **kill** under heuristic 1 ("closed basins are absolute kills").
- Source: USGS / Wikipedia / multiple; SEO "Know Your Basins — Great Divide Basin" (https://seo.wyo.gov/interstate-streams/know-your-basins/great-divide-basin) — page is JS-rendered nav; basin coordinator Mel Fegler, mel.fegler@wyo.gov, 307-777-7803.

## Green River Basin (Colorado River tributary)
- Green River is a major Colorado River tributary; WY is an Upper Basin headwaters state under the 1922 Colorado River Compact (7.5 MAF/yr to Upper Basin).
- Rock Springs + Green River share a municipal system (~39,000 residents) that would lose ~75% of its Green River appropriation in a curtailment (junior post-1922 rights cut first).
- Priority calls were employed in portions of the Green River (and Shoshone) basins during the 2020-2022 dry period.
- 2026: WY Senate File 0084 "Voluntary Water Conservation Program Act" (Select Water Committee) aims to shield junior users (municipalities, trona, refineries) from mandatory/uncompensated curtailment; SEO annual reports to Select Water Committee 2026-2031. State Engineer: Brandon Gebhart.
- Sources: WyoFile "Municipal water among most vulnerable in Colorado River crisis"; Casper Star-Tribune / Gillette / County17 (2026-02-19) on SF0084; Inside Climate News (2025-05); waterplan.state.wy.us Green River Basin compact memos.
