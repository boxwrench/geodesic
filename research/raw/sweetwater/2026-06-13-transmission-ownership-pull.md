## 1. TRANSMISSION — proximity read 🔍

**Anchor: Jim Bridger complex, ~10 mi N of Point of Rocks (NE of Rock Springs).** 🔍
- Jim Bridger Power Plant: 4 coal units, ~2,441 MW nameplate — one of the largest coal plants in the western US. Located ~10 mi N of Point of Rocks. (CLUI; gridinfo) 🔍
- Plant has its own switchyard/substation; sources reference 345 kV and the plant is the western terminus of the Gateway West D-segment lines. Exact bus voltages at the Jim Bridger switchyard (230/345/500) not cleanly confirmed in one primary doc — **gap**. 🔍

**Major HV lines/substations in or adjacent to the county:** 🔍
- **Gateway West (PacifiCorp/Energy Gateway):** Phase built 2019 = ~160 mi of **230 kV + 500 kV** connecting **Shirley Basin substation → Jim Bridger substation** ("D-segments"/Aeolus–Jim Bridger). Part of the ~990–1,000 mi Gateway West program adding 1,500–3,000 MW capacity across southern WY/ID. (BLM press release; SweetwaterNOW; County10) 🔍
- **Aeolus–Anticline 500 kV (140 mi):** new line from **Aeolus substation** (near Medicine Bow) to the new **Anticline substation** near the Jim Bridger plant, with a 5-mi segment Anticline→Jim Bridger substation. (Rocky Mountain Power; PAR Electric — line described as 500 kV/345 kV) 🔍
- **Gateway South 500 kV AC** (Aeolus → Clover sub near Mona, UT), path rating ~1,500 MW: **~34 mi routed through Sweetwater County** (108 mi through Carbon Co.), crossing checkerboard federal/state/private. (NS Energy; PacifiCorp) 🔍
- **PacifiCorp 230 kV Interconnectors Project (2018):** ~17.7 mi new/upgraded 230 kV around the **Aeolus / Shirley Basin / Difficulty–Miners / Freezeout / Standpipe** substations — mostly Carbon Co., east of Sweetwater. (BLM Rawlins FO news release, 4/17/2018) 🔍
- TransWest Express (Wyoming→Nevada DC line) often cited in same corridor; not confirmed crossing Sweetwater here — **gap**.

**Proximity verdict (vs. spec threshold "within ~10–15 mi of ≥115 kV"):** 🔍
- The **Rock Springs / Green River / Point of Rocks corridor (I-80 + UP rail)** sits adjacent to the Jim Bridger / Anticline 230–500 kV hub. Plausible large-site areas along the I-80 corridor in the eastern/central county are very likely **well inside the 10–15 mi / ≥115 kV threshold** — this looks like a PASS on transmission proximity, *pending* exact line geolocation. 🔍
- Caveat: proximity ≠ available interconnection capacity. Jim Bridger is a retiring/transitioning coal asset (Sen. Lummis "standing up for" it); queue/headroom is a separate diligence item — **gap / needs human (PacifiCorp OASIS / interconnection queue).**

## 2. LAND OWNERSHIP / CHECKERBOARD — the binding constraint 🔍

**County scale:** total area 10,491 sq mi (~6.71M ac); land 10,427 sq mi (~6.67M ac). Largest county in WY, 8th-largest in US. (Wikipedia/Census) 🔍

**The checkerboard (UP land grant, 1867):** 🔍
- ~50 mi wide × 150 mi long band of alternating 1-sq-mi (640-ac) sections along the I-80 / UP corridor across southern WY. (Cowboy State Daily) 🔍
- In the **Rock Springs area** specifically: checkerboard ~40 mi × 80 mi ≈ **2 million acres**, of which **~48% BLM** (~1M ac BLM) and ~1M ac private/other. (Rock Springs Grazing Assn interview, Western Confluence, Feb 2026) 🔍 — this is the best concrete federal-share figure found.
- Pattern is **"parallel estate"** (not split estate): federal surface + federal minerals on one section, private surface + private minerals on the adjacent section. (RSGA) 🔍

**Who owns the private half (CRITICAL for assembly):** 🔍
Original UP grant → Anadarko → Occidental → **Orion Mine Finance (2020, $1.33B)** for >1M fee surface ac + ~4–4.5M mineral ac across WY/UT/CO. Orion manages via **Sweetwater Royalties**; split into entities:
- **Aggie Grazing LLC** — surface/grazing (everything except trona)
- **Sweetwater Surface** — trona portion
- **Anadarko Land Corp** — much of oil & gas retained
- **Wildcat Coal** — coal properties
(Cowboy State Daily; Western Confluence; BusinessWire) 🔍

Original 2020 deal allocation by county: **~690,000 surface ac in Sweetwater Co.**, 130k Uinta, 110k Carbon, 70k Lincoln, smaller Albany/Laramie. (Cowboy State Daily) 🔍

**Are there contiguous ≥5,000-ac private blocks / large tracts for sale?** 🔍 — **This is the problem.**
- The private half of the Rock Springs checkerboard is **not freely contiguous** — it is, by construction, alternating 640-ac sections interlocked with BLM. True large *contiguous fee* blocks are limited; the practical "block" only exists through **lease aggregation** (RSGA controls the whole ~2M-ac unit via ownership + lease + BLM permit; RSGA itself holds ~520,000 ac of the private million, other 4 major owners ~480,000 ac). (RSGA) 🔍
- So a buyer can't just buy a clean 5,000-ac fee square in the checkerboard core; you'd assemble alternating sections from Aggie Grazing/Sweetwater + still face interlocked BLM sections (access easements not guaranteed; RSGA notes BLM lacks easements on many "BLM roads," counties often too). 🔍
- **Lead — large single-owner tracts for sale:** Sweetwater Royalties/Orion **has repeatedly offered the checkerboard lands**. State of WY bid in 2020 (lost to Orion), and in **July 2023 the state was again approached** by Sweetwater Royalties re: selling "parcels in the checkerboard." Extent/acreage/minerals of the current offering **unknown**. Orion also sold a 25% equity stake in Sweetwater Royalties to Ontario Teachers' Pension Plan (March 2023). (Cowboy State Daily, 7/11/2023) 🔍 → **This is the main acquisition pathway: a negotiated purchase from Orion/Sweetwater Royalties of an aggregated section block, not an MLS listing.** Needs human outreach.
- Off-checkerboard, large contiguous *fee* blocks are more likely **outside the railroad band** (e.g., away from I-80), but those tend to be farther from the Jim Bridger transmission hub — a likely transmission-vs-assembly tradeoff. 🔍

**Assembly verdict:** 🔍 Transmission proximity looks favorable along the I-80 corridor, but the checkerboard makes **clean contiguous private assembly the binding feasibility constraint.** Two realistic paths: (a) negotiate a multi-section block directly from Orion/Sweetwater Royalties / Aggie Grazing (lands have been on/off market 2020–2023); (b) pursue a BLM land exchange/consolidation to defragment a block (slow, federal, sage-grouse overlay risk). Either is a relationship/transaction problem, not a search-the-listings problem.
