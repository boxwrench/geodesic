=== ENERGY-SITING POSTURE (from appended Solar/WECS language amendment) ===
SOLAR Energy Facility standards (Sec 7.9): NOT within GMA; NOT within Greater Sage Grouse Core Areas (Gov Order 2011-5); NOT within wildlife migration corridors (EO 2020-1) absent WY Game&Fish-approved mitigation; min lot 40 ac; max height 25'; on-site lines underground.
WECS (wind, Sec 17): excluded from Wilderness Study Areas, ACECs, parks, historic trails, GMA; sage-grouse core exclusion; viewshed analysis (25 vantage points); WY Stat 18-5-504 compliance; heavy WY Game&Fish + federal (BLM/USFS/USFWS/BOR) coordination; significant-shadow-flicker cap 30 hrs/yr.
INTERPRETATION: utility-scale solar/wind is heavily wildlife-gated at the county level AND large generation also routes through WY Industrial Siting (state). County land-use "scale ceiling" for energy is constrained by sage-grouse/migration overlays, not board hostility.
