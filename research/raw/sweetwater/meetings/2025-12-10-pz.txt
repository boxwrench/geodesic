=== EMBEDDED SIGNED MINUTES: PZ Oct 8, 2025 (4 commissioners; Cordova excused) ===
Staff report accepted 4-0. Sept 10 2025 minutes approved 4-0.
PH#1 Adonis Bourdon & Eliza Frias - ZMA I-1 to RR, ~25.70 ac, Sec 23 T25N R106W. Applicant cleaned up property, lives in former-office mobile home. Staff rec approval. Doak moved approve. APPROVED 4-0. No opposition.
PH#2 Land Use Dept - Language Amendment Sec 7 (Solar CUP), 17 (WECS/wind), 21 (Definitions). Bingham: aligning solar+wind wildlife rules; solar/wind excluded from sage-grouse core + migration corridors absent WY G&F mitigation; "significant adverse impact" defined; MET tower standards (<=200', FAA, setback 1.5x height); KEY RATIONALE: "the only Solar project in Sweetwater County disrupted the migration of antelope. The applicant did not correlate with WY Game and Fish. Now these companies will need to meet with these agencies." County submitted comment on federal Sage Grouse RMP Amendment (favoring less-restrictive, aligning to Governor/State). Chair Lyon: WY G&F flagged "all the new projects coming to our county" - happy to be addressing it. Doak moved approve w/ MET-tower abandonment-removal addition. APPROVED 4-0.
Other Business: RMP Amendment scoping (yearlong); BCC signed letter supporting rescission of federal Conservation Rule.
