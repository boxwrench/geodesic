PH#1 Jared Triplett - CUP RV CAMPGROUND (11 sites + later admin bldg, ~1.01 ac), McKinnon, Agriculture zoning, intersection McKinnon Rd (CR-1) & US HWY 414.
  PUBLIC OPPOSITION (most contentious meeting in dataset): Petition of Dissent listing 10 concerns: (1) safety of children/school-bus stop, (2) trespassing across fields/env+property, (5) unfair burden on residents, (7) trespassing, (9) long-term occupancy / man-camp risk, (10) traffic congestion, plus fireworks/campfire, weak well water, accident potential on HWY 414, "peaceful rural character."

Father Mark Triplett spoke IN SUPPORT. Undersheriff Tomich: campgrounds increase calls-for-service; no resident deputies. State Reps Heiner & Brady wrote SUPPORT letters (tourism/econ dev).
  Applicant Jared Triplett addressed each concern (fencing 6-8' solid, secured dumpster, recreational-only, seasonal, working w/ Public Works on access; WYDOT closing HWY 414 access, using CR-1).
  Board: Richards pressed on man-camp/long-term -> applicant confirmed recreational; Gerrard: "understands neighborhood concerns however the property owner also has [rights]"; only 11 spots, valid concerns but manageable.
  Lyon moved to approve w/ staff recommendations. Jasperson seconded. APPROVED 4-0.
  Note: New campground/RV regulations to be heard in June (language amendment forthcoming).
