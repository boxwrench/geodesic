## 1. Greater sage-grouse — WY Core Areas (the dominant handle) 🔍

- WY administers sage-grouse via **Governor's Executive Order (current vintage EO 2019-3, "Greater Sage-Grouse Core Area Protection")**, not via ESA listing. Greater sage-grouse is **NOT** federally listed (warranted-but-precluded → withdrawn; remains a candidate-adjacent / BLM sensitive + state-policy species). So the handle is **state policy + BLM RMP stipulations**, layered onto federal land. 🔍
- Statewide, Core Areas are designed to capture **~80–84% of WY's sage-grouse population** while covering a much smaller share of land. 🔍
- **Sweetwater County is one of the most sage-grouse-constrained counties in WY.** It contains large designated Core Areas — the named **Salt Wells** Core Area sits in the county, and Core Area polygons blanket much of the checkerboard/Red Desert / Great Divide Basin terrain. 🔍
- **Rough county extent: HIGH.** Could not pull an exact county-clipped acreage/percent from a primary source in this pass. Qualitative read from BLM Rock Springs RMP materials + WGFD maps: a **large fraction of the county (plausibly on the order of half or more of the non-developed basin)** falls inside Core Area. **NEEDS exact GIS clip — flagged as human gap.** 🔍
- Core Area management constraints that bite a project: **5% surface-disturbance cap** per ~square-mile analysis area (1 well / 2.6 km²-equivalent density logic), **seasonal timing stipulations** (breeding/nesting), and **0.6-mile lek buffers / siting limits**. Statewide audits found realized disturbance averaging ~6.4% (range 0.7–18.7%) in monitored Core Areas, i.e. the cap is the operative siting constraint. 🔍

**Net:** sage-grouse Core Area is the #1 federal/state siting handle in Sweetwater. Treat as a hard siting-design constraint, not a kill — but it dictates layout, season-of-work, and density.

What a large Sweetwater project would *likely* encounter, from WY field-office / WYDOT / BLM secondary sources (NOT an official IPaC list): 🔍
- **Greater sage-grouse** — not ESA-listed; policy-constrained (see §1). Will dominate.
- **Downstream Colorado River endangered fish via water-depletion ESA §7:** **Colorado pikeminnow (E), razorback sucker (E), humpback chub (T), bonytail (E).** Sweetwater drains to the **Green/Colorado system**, and WY conserves these through a **depletion-fee / programmatic consultation on water depletions** — so any project with a federal nexus that *depletes water* triggers §7 even though the fish live downstream. This is a real, easy-to-trip handle for a water-using build. 🔍
- **Ute ladies'-tresses orchid (Spiranthes diluvialis, T)** — known from Sweetwater among other WY counties; riparian/wet-meadow habitat. NOTE: USFWS has *proposed delisting* (recovery) — status in flux. 🔍
- **Monarch butterfly** — candidate/proposed nationally; possible AOI hit (verify current status). 🔍
- **Migratory birds / Bald & Golden Eagle Protection Act** — eagles near-certain in this terrain; MBTA + BGEPA apply regardless of ESA. Golden eagle is a routine siting concern in WY basins. 🔍
- Blowout penstemon (E) and Colorado butterfly plant (T) are WY listed plants but are NOT Sweetwater (sandhills / Laramie-area) — likely not relevant. 🔍
- **Kendall Warm Springs dace (E)** is single-locality in **Sublette** County, not Sweetwater — not relevant. 🔍
- **Critical habitat:** no designated terrestrial critical habitat confirmed inside Sweetwater in this pass; downstream Colorado River fish critical habitat is outside the county. **Confirm via official IPaC AOI.** 🔍

## 4. WY Industrial Siting Act — state permitting handle (DEQ primary source, tagged 🔍 per Mode-B)

Source: WY DEQ Industrial Siting Division, Permitting page (jurisdictional thresholds). A facility needs an **Industrial Siting Council (ISC) permit** if: 🔍
- **Estimated construction cost ≥ $283,166,876** (inflation-indexed). Cost **$226,533,500–$283,166,876** → must obtain a **Certificate of Insufficient Jurisdiction**.
- **Wind: ≥ 20 towers** (all phases).
- **Solar: ≥ 30 MW rated capacity, OR ≥ 100 acres of surface disturbance** (all phases).
- Regardless of cost: large commercial waste incineration/disposal (>500 short tons/day), regulated hazardous-waste commercial facilities, commercial radioactive-waste facilities, and county-referred wind/solar projects (W.S. 18-5-509).
- ISC has a **165-day statutory clock** to approve/deny.

**Net:** any utility-scale generation or large industrial build in Sweetwater almost certainly **triggers ISC review** — solar ≥30 MW or ≥100 ac, wind ≥20 towers, or cost ≥ ~$283M. This is a real **state** permitting handle (socio-economic + environmental review), distinct from federal. Note WY also delegates wind/solar siting partly to **county commissioners (W.S. 18-5-502)** with referral-up to ISC.
