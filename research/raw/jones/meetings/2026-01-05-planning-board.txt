JONES COUNTY PLANNING BOARD — SPECIAL MEETING — Monday, January 5, 2026, 5:00 pm
Location: Conference Room, Administrative Building, 110 S Market Street, Trenton, NC 28585.

Agenda items (ordinance-text amendments — NOT applications):
- Amendments to the Subdivision Ordinance and Session Law 1999-125
- Amendments to the Manufactured Home Park Ordinance
- Addition of a Recreational Vehicle (RV) Park Ordinance
