## STAGE 1 — Discover (host = capacity signal)

- 🔍 **Host:** self-hosted **WordPress / Elementor** site at `jonescountync.gov`. Agendas + minutes are **individual Google Drive PDFs** (e.g. `drive.google.com/file/d/…`) plus a **Google Drive embedded-folder archive** ("prior to March 2023"). Meeting **video is on Vimeo** (showcase `vimeo.com/showcase/jonescountycommissioners`) + **Facebook Live**. Site notes: *"We are transitioning to a new agenda software."* **No Granicus / Legistar / structured API.** Google-Drive-as-records-system + Vimeo + "transitioning software" = **low-capacity clerk tier** — lower than Sweetwater (Revize + Granicus), comparable to / slightly above Fall River (PDF clerk site). 
  - **Capacity tell:** the Planning Board is administered through the **Economic Development Commission / County Manager's Office** (Governing Boards page: *"Contact Jones County EDC … for more information"* re the Planning Board; agenda items go to the County Manager's clerk). **No standalone full-time Planning Director / Planning Department** is listed in the department roster (there is an **Inspections** dept and an **Economic Development** dept, but no Planning dept). Planning is a part-time function bolted onto EDC + Manager. Strong low-capacity signal.
- 🔍 **NO COUNTY ZONING is the headline Stage-1 finding (matches Fall River pattern).** The **Jones County Land Use Plan 2013-2033** states verbatim (NCDOT-hosted mirror, p.49):
  > *"Trenton, Maysville, and Pollocksville all have zoning (but no extraterritorial jurisdictions) that are administered independent of the County. **The County does not have any form of zoning.**"*
  and (p.4): *"A land use plan has no designated authority to determine how an individual chooses to use his or her property. A zoning ordinance would be a completely separate document. **If at any time zoning was initiated**, there would be public newspaper notices … Commissioners would have the final say…"* (i.e., zoning is hypothetical/future, not in force).
- 🔍 **What the county DOES regulate** (the actual land-use touchpoints): (a) **county-wide Subdivision Regulations** (lot size, setbacks, density; plats reviewed preliminary + final by the Planning Board), modified by **Session Law 1999-125** (a local act that **exempts divisions into parcels >5 acres with no right-of-way dedication** from subdivision review — a pro-large-tract carve-out); (b) **Manufactured Home Park Ordinance**; (c) **Flood Damage Prevention** (Pollocksville flood ordinance is the only flood doc listed; county-level floodplain handled via state/FEMA); (d) **solar site-plan review** (Timber Mill Solar amendment came to the PB); (e) a new **RV Park Ordinance** under development (Jan 2026). Septic/well = state. Building permits = **county Inspections dept** (state code).
- 🔍 **ETJ exposure = ZERO on unincorporated land.** Per the Land Use Plan, the three towns (Trenton/Maysville/Pollocksville) zone **but have NO extraterritorial jurisdiction**. GS 160D-202 *permits* up to ~1-mi ETJ, but Jones's towns have **not exercised it**. A county-unincorporated AOI therefore has **no municipal ETJ overlay** to clear. (This resolves the Stage-1 ETJ open item.)
- 🔍 **Land Use Plan recency + posture:** **adopted 2013, horizon 2013-2033** (now ~13 yrs old, mid-life; not stale, but pre-160D recodification). Posture = **pro-orderly-growth but advisory only**: *"Identify areas primed for growth," "effective, responsible, orderly growth,"* yet explicitly *"the Land Use Plan is not a land use control in any way — the Land Use Plan serves as a guide."* Build-out analysis: unincorporated Jones could theoretically hold **~881,000–1,029,469 people** ("maximum potential … under current regulations") — i.e., **no regulatory scarcity / no density ceiling binding** at our scale. Existing land use: **>95% undeveloped, 49.4% agricultural**, 3.5% residential.
- Population ~9,172 (2020). Cadence: **Commissioners** meet 1st Monday (regular) + 3rd Monday (workshop) monthly; **Planning Board** meets **only when applications/ordinance work require it** (special-called meetings; ~quarterly in the record, 5–6 pm).

### ⚠ L15 CAVEAT — the grade is conditional on topology and is INVALIDATED as a "friendly board" reading
🔍 **Jones County has NO county zoning.** Per the L15 rule, an 86 here means the **same thing it meant for Fall River (88.5): the absence of a gate, not the presence of a pro-growth machine.** At the county level there is **nothing to deny** for non-subdivision uses, so the meeting record carries **little predictive signal for a large/novel project** (new city, utility-scale industrial). The binding constraints on a large Jones entry are **NOT the Planning Board** but: (a) **wetlands/§404 + NEPA** (Stage-1 kill-eligible layer — Croatan ~75% wetlands, Hofmann Forest, pocosins); (b) **CCPCUA groundwater cap** (capacity-use permit, state objective to *reduce* withdrawals); (c) **state/utility infrastructure** (Duke / Jones-Onslow EMC). The high grade should be read as **"low-capacity, low-friction, low-information"** — identical framing to Fall River.

## Comparison to Sweetwater (84.5 / Boom) & Fall River (88.5 / Wild West)
🔍 **Jones ≈ Fall River, not Sweetwater.** All three are high-friendliness rural counties, but:
- **Sweetwater (84.5):** a *real, active* P&Z that approves ~86–96% — a measured friendliness of an institution that **does** gate development (Boom County / high-capacity).
- **Fall River (88.5):** **no zoning at all**; 100% of ministerial land-use actions approved; the grade reflects **structural absence of a gate** (Wild West / low-capacity). Not a pro-growth machine.
- **Jones (≈86):** **no county zoning either**, BUT — unlike Fall River — Jones has a **functioning 7-member Planning Board that does subdivision-plat + solar site-plan review** and is actively **adding ordinances** (RV parks). So Jones sits **between Fall River and Sweetwater on capacity**: more land-use machinery than Fall River, far less than Sweetwater's professional P&Z, and **no discretionary zoning** like Fall River. Topology = **Wild West leaning toward low-end Gatekeeper** (it has a plat-review gate but no zoning gate). The Jones grade is **lower-confidence** than both comparators because **outcomes/minutes were not retrievable** (Google-Drive/Vimeo gap), whereas Sweetwater (embedded minutes) and Fall River (indexed PDF minutes) both had parseable outcomes.
- **Ranking is fragile and within noise.** Treat all three as "high-friendliness"; do not rank Jones precisely against the others.

## Candidate friendliness markers observed (for the marker loop)
1. 🔍 **"No county zoning + a working subdivision/site-plan Planning Board" as a distinct topology cell** — between Fall River's total absence and Sweetwater's full P&Z. Marker: *a county can have a Planning Board and a Land Use Plan and still have NO zoning; the existence of a "Planning Board" does NOT imply a discretionary rezoning gate — check the Land Use Plan's "what does this have to do with zoning?" section.* (Recurs across NC small counties.)
2. 🔍 **Local-act subdivision exemption (Session Law 1999-125: >5-acre splits, no ROW dedication, exempt)** — a pro-large-tract carve-out that makes large rural assembly *less* regulated, not more. Candidate non-friction marker for NC counties.
3. 🔍 **Land-use plan that disclaims being a "land use control"** — explicit advisory-only language ("not a land use control in any way") is a strong friendliness/low-friction tell, parallel to Fall River's "we don't regulate" posture.
4. 🔍 **Utility-scale solar as the de-facto scale ceiling in no-zoning ag counties** — when there's no zoning, the only large project that routinely appears is solar (site-plan review). 80 MW Timber Mill + Trent River Solar. Marker: *in no-zoning Southeast ag counties, solar site-plan review is the meeting-record's best proxy for "how does this board treat a large entrant?"*
5. 🔍 **Planning function run out of the EDC / Manager's office (no Planning Director)** — capacity-and-hunger tell: low institutional capacity but development-recruitment-oriented (Planning Board contact IS the Economic Development Commission).
