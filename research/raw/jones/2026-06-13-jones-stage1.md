# Jones County, NC — Stage 1 cache

**Compiled:** 2026-06-13 · backs Stage 1 cells in results/nc-jones/county-jones.md. Pre-pilot: kills recorded as data.

## Wetlands / §404 (Southeast kill-first layer) — severe
- Croatan National Forest spans 167,687 ac across Craven, Carteret, Jones; ~75% of the Croatan is wetlands (pocosins dominate). Only true coastal forest in the East.
- Jones also includes Great Dover Swamp, White Oak Pocosin, and Hofmann Forest (state-owned, "nation's largest forest laboratory").
- Trent River + tributaries (Island Creek, Reedy Branch) → floodplain. Coastal-plain hydrology.
- Implication: individual §404 permit + NEPA very likely on critical path; near framework/03 "pervasive wetlands at all candidate sites" Southeast kill trigger.
- Sources: USFS Croatan; NCpedia Croatan + Jones County; Wikipedia Croatan/Jones; NC Coastal Land Trust (Island Creek).

## Scale-threshold
- Jones County pop ~9,172 (2020); 4th-least-populous NC county. Towns: Trenton (seat), Pollocksville, Maysville (largest).
- 50k build-out ≈ 5.5x county; entry 2-5k = ratio ~0.2-0.5. Past >1.0 kill-review at build-out (heuristic 3).
- Sources: Wikipedia/Data USA/NCpedia Jones County; 2020 Census.

## Ownership / assembly
- Historic plantation-scale private tracts (large) interrupted by Croatan NF + Hofmann Forest (state) + Coastal Land Trust conservation holdings. Dry + large + contiguous + non-wetland intersection is the hard part.

## Water (confirms state screen)
- Jones in Central Coastal Plain Capacity Use Area (CCPCUA); groundwater >100k gal/day needs capacity-use permit; state objective is to REDUCE withdrawals to sustainable levels. New-city demand (~6-9 MGD) lands in a capped basin.

## Transmission / utilities
- Jones-Onslow EMC (Trenton industrial park); Duke Energy (Maysville, Pollocksville). Coastal grid denser than SW SD; likely adequate. 🔍 HIFLD.

## Federal
- §404 dominant handle; coastal-plain listed species plausible (e.g., red-cockaded woodpecker in pine). Worst federal-handle posture of the three counties.
