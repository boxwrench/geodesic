## Water
- Hot Springs and western SD/WY communities rely on the Madison aquifer (confined Black Hills karst aquifer); declining water levels flagged as a major regional issue (USGS Karst Aquifers: Madison).
- SD doctrine: prior appropriation; permit via DANR Chief Engineer / Water Management Board; 5 yr to construct + 4 to beneficial use.
- Surface: Cheyenne River drainage. 🔍 DANR appropriation status for the candidate source.

## Tribal / federal
- Pine Ridge (Oglala Lakota) Indian Reservation bounds the grassland area to the south; regional Tribal Energy Access Corridor (Cheyenne River–Pine Ridge–Rosebud–Standing Rock–Yankton) mostly along existing ROW.
- USFS grassland presence → a federal land handle (exchange/special-use permit) likely on the critical path → NEPA + §106. Worse federal posture than Sweetwater's BLM checkerboard.
