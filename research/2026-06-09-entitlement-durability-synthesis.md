# Research note: Entitlement durability — two-agent exercise

**Date:** 2026-06-09
**Method:** The [core question](../framework/00-the-question.md) was posed cold to two independent LLM agents with no project context, to test whether the question alone elicits useful structure. It did. This note records what was adopted into the framework, and the caveats.

## Agent 1 — The evidence ladder (California-framed)

Anchored in CA law (CEQA, development agreements, vesting tentative maps). Key generalizable insight:

> The cheapest *meaningful* evidence is a public, legally operative act — not staff enthusiasm, mayor meetings, or EDO letters. Those prove interest, not durability.

Its evidence ladder, generalized: verbal support → counsel opinion that the pathway exists → public council direction to negotiate → adopted term sheet/reimbursement agreement → adopted entitlement with challenge windows expired → recorded DA + vested map with referendum/litigation windows cleared. The first item worth underwriting is the last one.

**Adopted:** for each pilot state, define the "first legally operative act" and the "full durable package" so the funnel's Commit stage has a concrete, state-specific finish line. Also: never rely on a friendly *initiative* as a vesting mechanism (CA courts: DAs can't be adopted by initiative).

## Agent 2 — Regime-first analysis

The higher-value response. Reframed "where" as a state-law question before a county question.

**Adopted into [02 — State Screen](../framework/02-state-screen.md):**

1. Four statute-readable variables: vesting timing (early-statutory vs. reliance rule), referendum reach over the durability instrument, mini-NEPA private right of action, boundary gatekeeper.
2. The decisive conjunction: mini-NEPA + referendum reach = two independent kill channels requiring no unwilling individual. Structurally California; detectable for $0.
3. Tentative state rankings: TX near-best (Ch. 245, no mini-NEPA, MUDs, friendly annexation); UT/AZ second tier (AZ Prop 207 makes downzoning compensable).

**Adopted into [00 — The Question](../framework/00-the-question.md):**

4. The breach test: what claim accrues on revocation, and is it enjoinable or damages-only? Próspera is the proof case — a 50-year ZEDE stability guarantee, repealed 2022, remedy is a ~$10.7B ICSID arbitration for damages. Damages-only durability = removable but compensated.

**Adopted into [05 — Entitlement Paths](../framework/05-entitlement-paths.md):**

5. Path-as-variable: county entitlement vs. annexation vs. incorporation vs. special district are different veto stacks over the same parcel (California Forever's Suisun City pivot is the empirical proof).
6. "Buy already-entitled" as a standing parallel track.
7. Reedy Creek lesson: legislatively created districts are exposed to state turnover unless revocation is compensable/enjoinable.

**Also noted:** federal "Freedom Cities" route stalled and executive-order-durability is the weakest instrument class; the honest base-rate caveat that a cheap durable yes at 10–100x magnitude may not exist anywhere — in which case the funnel's correct output is "buy already-entitled" as the only live option.

## Caveats

- **All statutory claims are unverified hypotheses.** Some citations in the raw outputs were weak (including non-authoritative sources). Vesting, referendum reach, and DA classification are state- and sometimes county-specific. Local land-use counsel is the source of truth; the screen generates their questions.
- **Neither agent assessed WY, SD, or NC** — the actual pilot states. The four-variable screen has never been run on them, while it tentatively crowns Texas, which is not on the current candidate list. Running the pilot states through the screen is the first action item ([ROADMAP](../ROADMAP.md)).
- Agent 1's ladder is CA-specific in its particulars; only the structure generalizes.
