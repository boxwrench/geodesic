# Roadmap

Principle: **earn the engine.** No automation is built until a manual pilot proves which signals discriminate. The pilot is the three counties currently under consideration: Sweetwater WY, Fall River SD, Jones NC.

**Pre-pilot rule — collect data, don't eliminate.** During pre-pilot and the pilot the goal is *data and feedback*, not winnowing. So a 🔴 is **recorded as a finding** (with the evidence the principal would want to judge whether to pursue) but **does not stop the screen** — every signal runs on every pilot county regardless of would-kill results, because a signal that never gets exercised can't be calibrated ([Step 2](ops/step-2-calibration.md)), and a county dropped early stops generating the comparative data the pilot exists to produce. Real elimination (acting on kills) begins only when the funnel goes live post-pilot. The [kill-override](framework/03-county-screen.md#kill-override--a-kill-is-a-hypothesis-not-a-verdict) is the live-mode escape valve; the pre-pilot rule is its pilot-mode counterpart — here, *all* kills are effectively held open by default.

Each step has a detailed, hand-off-ready game plan in [ops/](ops/README.md), with every task tagged by execution mode (automated / AI-assisted / human-on-computer / human-offline) and sourced from the [data-source registry](ops/data-sources.md).

## Standing checks

- **Source-annotation / truth-audit rule:** every claim in `results/` is annotated to a specific *official* primary source (section + quoted operative phrase) and tagged with its audit status — ✅ verified against the official source · 🔍 draft from a secondary/finding-aid source · ❓ open question. Un-annotated claims don't count as findings. Full standard: [ops/README — execution modes & governing rules](ops/README.md#execution-modes).
- Every statutory claim remains a hypothesis until counsel-reviewed (flag in state files); counsel review operates on top of ✅, not in place of it.
- Every scorecard cell traceable to a sourced, audit-tagged finding in its locale file.
- **Capture transferable lessons as you go:** every locale study logs anything that generalizes to the next one — a signal that did/didn't discriminate, a data source that lied or delivered, a framework procedure that was wrong — in [research/pilot-lessons.md](research/pilot-lessons.md). The pilot's deliverable is the method improvements, not just three filled-in files.
