# 00 — The Question

Every signal, screen, and scorecard in this repo exists to answer one question. If a piece of work doesn't serve it, the work is decoration.

## The real question

> **Where can we obtain entitlements for a project 10–100x larger than anything the jurisdiction has ever approved, and have those entitlements be *durable* — binding enough to survive political turnover, organized opposition, and a ballot initiative — before we've spent serious money?**

The screening form, which is what the elimination engine actually computes:

> **What is the cheapest observable evidence that this jurisdiction can grant — and is legally able to keep — a yes of this magnitude?**

## How this differs from the naive question

The naive question is "where is rezoning easy?" That misses three things:

**Ease vs. durability.** A friendly county can say yes quickly and then un-say it: new commissioners, a citizen referendum, a zoning rewrite triggered by your own project. The approval must become *property* — vested rights, a statutory development agreement, contractual annexation terms. That is mostly state law, not county vibes. California Forever's problem was never unfriendly county staff; it's that California gives opponents a ballot-box veto over the very instrument that creates durability.

**Willingness vs. capacity vs. authority.** Watching planning meetings measures *willingness*. A durable yes also requires *capacity* (can a two-person planning office process a new-city application at all?) and *authority* (does the county legally control the decision, or do state agencies, neighboring municipalities, or voters hold a veto?). A county can be 10/10 willing and structurally unable to deliver.

**Sequencing risk.** The question is explicitly about *certainty per dollar at each stage of spend*. Every signal we collect is judged by one metric: does it change the estimate of durable-yes probability per dollar spent?

## The breach test

The deepest version of "can it keep the yes" is a single statute-reading question:

> **If a later hostile actor revokes the instrument, what claim accrues — and does that claim yield an injunction or specific performance, or only money?**

A stability guarantee on paper is a worthless surface signal. Próspera (Honduras) held a 50-year legal-stability guarantee embedded in the ZEDE framework; the framework was repealed in 2022 anyway, and the remedy is a decade-long arbitration for *damages*. Damages-only durability means you can be removed — you just get paid. If the goal is a city that exists rather than a settlement check, the instrument must vest as a property or contract interest whose breach is enjoinable, and it must vest *before* opposition organizes.
