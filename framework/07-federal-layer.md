# 07 — The Federal Layer

The [state screen](02-state-screen.md) hunts for states without mini-NEPA litigation vetoes — but **federal law follows the project into every state.** A "no-mini-NEPA" state is not a no-NEPA state: NEPA, the Clean Water Act, and the ESA attach through *federal handles*, and the strategic rule is therefore:

> **Minimize federal handles.** Every federal permit, approval, or dollar is a hook for NEPA review and federal-court litigation, regardless of how friendly the state is.

## The main federal veto holders

| Authority | Trigger | What it means for us |
|---|---|---|
| **CWA §404** (Army Corps) | Filling wetlands / "waters of the United States" | The big one, especially in eastern NC. An *individual* permit means public-interest review, likely NEPA analysis, and a litigation surface; *nationwide* permits are fast but capped at small impacts. WOTUS scope is in regulatory flux — current rule must be checked at execution time. |
| **ESA** (USFWS) | Listed species / critical habitat on site | §7 consultation if any federal nexus exists; §10 habitat-conservation plan (slow, expensive) if not. |
| **NEPA** | Any federal handle: §404 individual permit, federal funding, federal land, FERC interconnection facilities, etc. | The mini-NEPA screen's logic, federalized. The handle, not the project, determines exposure. |
| **NHPA §106** | Federal handle + historic/cultural resources | Tribal consultation; pairs with the tribal-lands Stage 1 check. |
| **Others (usually minor here)** | FAA (airport/tall structures), STB (new rail), Clean Air Act (nonattainment areas) | Screen-level only; rural sites rarely bind. |

## How this enters the funnel

- **Site selection is the mitigation.** The cheapest §404 strategy is a site that isn't wet: wetlands density (NWI) and stream networks are already Stage 1 signals — this doc explains *why they're a federal-litigation proxy, not just a buildability proxy.*
- **Stage 1 additions:** USFWS **IPaC** report (free, per-AOI, scriptable) for listed species + critical habitat; NWI density as §404-exposure proxy.
- **Stage 3 question:** can the project's footprint and utilities plausibly proceed on *nationwide* permits and avoid an individual §404 permit? If not, add 2–4+ years and a NEPA process to the timeline model.
- **Path interaction:** federal funding (infrastructure grants, etc.) is a NEPA handle — taking federal money for offsite infrastructure can import the very review regime the state screen selected against. Price that trade explicitly.

## Honest scope note

At city scale, *some* federal touchpoint is near-certain eventually. The realistic goal is not zero federal exposure but keeping federal handles off the **critical path** of the first phases — entry below the threshold, again ([heuristic 3](01-heuristics.md)).
