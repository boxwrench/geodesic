# 03 — The County Screen

Counties that survive the [state screen](02-state-screen.md) enter the elimination funnel. The goal of every stage is to **kill the candidate**, not confirm it. Customize the kill criteria per region ([heuristic 1](01-heuristics.md)), never the stages.

## The phase-gate funnel

| Stage | Time box | Key question | Example kill triggers |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Screen | 2 hours | Existential physical/regulatory constraints? | Closed water basin (West); pervasive wetlands at all candidate sites (Southeast); tribal/military overlays; no parcel assembly path |
| 2. Filter | 1 day | Regulatory topology and political capacity? | Hostile planner; pending zoning rewrite; no PUD/special-use framework; litigious public-comment history |
| 3. Validate | 1 week | Physical/economic reality of extending services? | No water source within economic pipeline distance; no path to high-voltage transmission; severe geological constraints |
| 4. Commit | 1 month | Who are the veto players and what do they want? | Organized funded opposition; environmental NGO interest; split commission with election inside 18 months |

## The county topology matrix

Classify within the first 30 minutes:

|  | High capacity | Low capacity |
|---|---|---|
| **Low friction** | **The Boom County** — scaled precedents, routine permitting. *Optimal entry.* | **The Wild West** — no zoning today, no staff for scale. *Late bottlenecks; zero path dependency (they can adopt hostile zoning in response to you).* |
| **High friction** | **The Gatekeeper** — professional staff, predictable process, every move contested. | **The Bottleneck** — high rules, low staff, chronic paralysis. *Avoid.* |

Classify via: (1) full-time planning director or part-time clerk? (2) largest project on the last 12 months of agendas? (3) is the CUP/PUD procedure three paragraphs or three pages?

## Rezoning-ease metrics (first-class measurements)

The directly measurable proxies for "ease of rezoning and permitting" — collect these for every county that reaches Stage 2:

1. **Rezoning approval rate** over the last 24 months of minutes (approved / heard).
2. **Median application-to-decision time** for rezonings and CUPs.
3. **Largest project approved in 5 years** (acres, units, or jobs) — the scale-threshold denominator.
4. **PUD procedure existence and length** — page count is a friction proxy.
5. **Comp plan recency and growth posture** — when last updated; does it designate growth areas or defend against them?

## Vibes-to-data translation

Qualitative signals are high-value but must be translated into structural facts:

| Vibe signal | What it actually means | Verification step |
|---|---|---|
| Planner: "We'd love to have you!" | Wants jobs/tax base. Says nothing about capacity. | Ask: largest subdivision or industrial park approved in 5 years, and how long did review take? |
| Agendas dominated by roads, bridges, budgets | Land use isn't politicized. Very good. | Confirm a zoning ordinance/building dept exists at all |
| Public comment packed with variance protests | High social-license risk; organized opposition will form. | Discard early, or budget a multi-year political campaign |
| EDO is part-time/volunteer/regional | Low institutional capacity — but possibly high hunger. Tiebreaker, not filter. | Assume you bring your own infrastructure, legal drafts, coalition |
| No zoning in unincorporated areas | Low friction today, zero path dependency. | Check state enabling statutes: how fast can the board adopt zoning in response to you? |

## Stealth diligence (asymmetric information)

What counties and landowners know but won't disclose:

1. **State engineer protest log** (prior-appropriation states): if irrigators protest every minor municipal water application in the basin, expect years of delay.
2. **24-month minutes deep-dive**: search "economic development," "subdivision," "water" in minutes from 12 and 24 months ago — the political baseline before you showed up.
3. **Litigation scrape**: county name + "lawsuit" + "development"/"water rights." Active developer litigation = litigious arena.
4. **Tax-base concentration**: single-industry counties see you as either salvation or existential threat. No middle ground — determine which before the first call.
5. **Referendum/election history**: has any large project in this county ever been put to or killed by a ballot measure? Board flip frequency and margins.
6. **Standing opposition organizations**: a slow-growth or land-trust litigant with a win record (the Greenbelt Alliance pattern) is a $0-detectable structural veto.

## Weak signals — tiebreakers only

Meeting frequency, EDO staffing level, and general "friendliness" are noisy. They break ties between counties that already passed structural screens; they never gate.
