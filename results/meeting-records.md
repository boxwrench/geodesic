# Meeting-Record Sources — keep resource

A persistent registry of **where each county's meeting records actually live** — the output of [meeting-grading Stage 1 (Discover)](../ops/protocol-meeting-grading.md). These links are a reusable asset: they're the entry point for the friendliness grade, they get re-checked every time we re-run a county, and the **host tier is itself a capacity signal** ([pilot lesson L2](../research/pilot-lessons.md)). Always link this registry from a county's report rather than re-pasting URLs in prose.

**Host tier (capacity proxy, best → weakest):** `Legistar/Granicus video` ▸ `YouTube` ▸ `PDF agendas/minutes` ▸ `records request only`. Audit markers per the [source-annotation rule](../ops/README.md#execution-modes).
