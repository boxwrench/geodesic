## Verdict

**Stage 1 (2026-06-13, draft; pre-pilot = all signals run, kills as data):** Jones is **🔴-leaning, and — importantly for the pilot — killed by a completely different layer than the Western counties.** The Southeast kill-first layer is **wetlands/§404**, and Jones is close to the framework's "pervasive wetlands at all candidate sites" kill trigger: the county is dominated by the **Croatan National Forest** (~75% wetlands/pocosin), the **Great Dover Swamp**, **White Oak Pocosin**, and the state-owned **Hofmann Forest**. A new-city footprint here would very likely need an **individual §404 permit + NEPA** on the critical path (the [federal layer](../../framework/07-federal-layer.md) risk, realized). Second hard flag: **scale** — county pop **~9,172** (4th-least-populous in NC), so 50k build-out ≈ 5.5x the county ([heuristic 3](../../framework/01-heuristics.md) kill-review). Plus **CCPCUA water** capping (per [state screen](state-nc.md)) and large federal/state forest blocks complicating assembly. **Offsetting:** NC's *governance* is the most favorable of the three (statutory vesting, no referendum, defanged SEPA — [state screen](state-nc.md)) — but governance strength cannot rescue a site that fails the physical §404 layer ([heuristic 1](../../framework/01-heuristics.md): fall in love at infrastructure, kill at the constraint layer). **Net for the principal:** Jones is the clearest physical-constraint case of the three — strong law, wrong dirt. Recorded as data, not eliminated (pilot rule). **Pilot value:** Jones makes the region-conditional kill-ordering ([heuristic 1](../../framework/01-heuristics.md)) *real* — wetlands/§404 discriminated here exactly as water did in the West.

**Stage 2 (🔍 draft, 2026-06-13):** Topology **Wild West→low-end Gatekeeper** — **no county zoning** (Land Use Plan: *"the County does not have any form of zoning"*) but a working 7-member Planning Board does plats + solar site-plans. Friendliness grade **86/100 — but L15 caveat INVALIDATES it as "friendly board"**: like Fall River's 88.5, it scores high from *absence of a gate*, not a pro-growth machine. ETJ exposure **zero** (towns don't exercise ETJ). The binding constraints remain Stage-1 physical (§404, CCPCUA, scale), not the board. Detail: [Stage 2 below](#stage-2--filter-1d-budget-actual-1d-mode-b-draft-2026-06-13).
