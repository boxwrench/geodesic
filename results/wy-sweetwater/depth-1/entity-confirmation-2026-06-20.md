## Why This Matters

The first landholder conversation should not say "we want Sweetwater County land." It should say:

> We have identified specific assessor parcels under Sweetwater-platform owner names, led by `SWEETWATER SURFACE LLC` and `AGGIE GRAZING LLC`, and want to discuss whether a 5,000-10,000 acre Green River / I-80 corridor surface block could be optioned or purchased without disturbing Sweetwater's mineral and royalty strategy.

## Source Evidence

### Sweetwater corporate ownership and transaction status

Sweetwater's own website says it owns roughly 850,000 fee surface acres and more than 4.5M mineral acres, and says the company is primarily owned by Orion Resource Partners and Ontario Teachers' Pension Plan.

URC's April 16, 2026 transaction release says:

- pro forma URC would become the largest landowner in Wyoming, with about 850,000 fee surface acres and about 4.5M mineral acres;
- Sweetwater owns about 200,000 acres inside the KSLA plus about 650,000 additional fee surface acres;
- Sweetwater remains primarily owned by Orion and Ontario Teachers pre-closing;
- shareholder meeting is expected around July 2026, with closing thereafter subject to required approvals;
- expected closing is around early Q3 2026;
- Sweetwater will continue under CEO Damon Barber after completion.

Depth 1 implication: the transaction improves the likely future decision path, but surface-sale authority should be treated as unsettled until closing is confirmed.
