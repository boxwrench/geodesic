# Sweetwater Depth 1 Water Path

Date: 2026-06-20  
Status: initial Depth 1 water packet

## Decision Question

Can the Sweetwater corridor site secure drought-firm water for entry and preserve a credible build-out path without relying only on a new junior Green River right?

## Current Answer

Entry looks feasible if the site is on the Green River / Rock Springs / I-80 corridor. Build-out is not an availability problem as much as a firmness problem. The project should treat a new right as useful but insufficient; the real build-out path is Fontenelle/storage or acquisition/conversion of a senior right.

## Source Findings

- BLM approved the Dry Creek Trona Project in Sweetwater County in May 2025, including major industrial facilities and water infrastructure. Local Stage 3 analysis treats the associated Green River water right as build-out-scale precedent, but the exact water-right record still needs primary SEO confirmation.
- Reclamation's Fontenelle operations page is the stable federal source path for reservoir status and operations, but it does not answer uncommitted M&I contract availability.
- Wyoming SEO e-Permit is the correct path for water-right details and senior-right searches. This remains a browser/manual pull.

## Firmness Logic

The project should separate three questions:

1. Is water physically present near the corridor? Current answer: yes.
2. Can a new right be obtained? Current answer: probably yes, supported by recent industrial precedent.
3. Is that right reliable in drought/compact stress? Current answer: not by itself.

That makes the next move a storage/senior-right search, not another generic "is there water in Sweetwater" memo.

Water supports a Sweetwater corridor search. It does not support a Great Divide Basin interior search unless the principal is willing to underwrite desalination/brine-disposal risk.
