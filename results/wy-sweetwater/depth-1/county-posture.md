Date: 2026-06-20  
Status: initial posture packet from existing meeting and county-screen work

## Decision Question

Is Sweetwater County likely to support a real site-specific development agreement once the parcel, water, and power strategy are visible?

## Current Answer

Probably yes, but county support is not the binding constraint. The county appears pro-growth and industrially fluent; the hard vetoes are the landholder, BLM/RMP/sage-grouse overlays, water firmness, and PacifiCorp/PSC at build-out scale.

## Working Posture

Treat Sweetwater County as a necessary ally, not the controlling gate. A county development agreement should be prepared only after an actual block, water path, and power approach are defined. Asking the county to bless an abstract new-city concept would spend political capital before the hard constraints are real.
