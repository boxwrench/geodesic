# Sweetwater WY — Site-Focused Search Addendum

Date: 2026-06-20  
Status: draft; post-sync focused search  
Scope: actual Sweetwater target as currently defined in the repo: a 5,000-10,000 acre entry block from the Sweetwater Royalties / New URC surface estate on the Green River / I-80 corridor. Exact parcel polygon is still not fixed.

## Bottom Line

The new focus strengthens the Sweetwater recommendation, but narrows it: do not search "Sweetwater County" generally. Search for a corridor parcel package that simultaneously passes four gates:

1. Green River side, not Great Divide Basin interior.
2. Sweetwater Royalties / New URC surface tract, preferably acquired by private purchase rather than BLM exchange.
3. Outside the worst Rock Springs RMP / ACEC / sage-grouse constraint overlays.
4. Below the 200 MW PacifiCorp cliff at entry, with a paid study before any build-out promise.

This is no longer a county-screen problem. It is a parcel-package and counterparty problem.

## What Changed With The New Focus

### Power

Self-built power changes the interpretation but not the gate. Weak grid service is not a kill if generation is self-built, but the project still needs to resolve:

- interconnection if generation is grid-connected;
- transmission service or private-wire feasibility;
- PacifiCorp large-load treatment if any load is served through Rocky Mountain Power;
- Wyoming PSC / special-contract exposure at or above 200 MW.

Rocky Mountain Power's current large-service page sets Wyoming thresholds at 25-199 MW and greater than 200 MW, with greater-than-200 MW requiring a special contract, incremental generation requirements, commission approval, and full project-cost security. That means "build our own power" should be treated as a project strategy, not as a reason to ignore the 200 MW cliff.

Immediate action: keep entry load below 25 MW if possible; if not, commission the system-impact study before any land option is sized around build-out demand.

Sources: Rocky Mountain Power large service requests; `research/raw/sweetwater/2026-06-13-stage3-transmission.md`.

### Water

The earlier water finding is still directionally right, but the key distinction is now sharper:

- new Green River rights appear obtainable at scale, as shown by the approved Dry Creek / Pacific Soda project south of Green River;
- new rights are junior and curtailment-exposed;
- build-out needs firming through Fontenelle M&I storage or acquisition/conversion of a senior right;
- corridor siting matters because Green River / Rock Springs / I-80 conveyance passes, while Great Divide Basin interior siting pushes toward saline groundwater and desalination.

This means water is not "unavailable." The real question is whether the project can own or contract drought-firm supply before making a build-out commitment.

Immediate action: open the Fontenelle / WWDC / Reclamation conversation before land option spend. In parallel, identify acquirable senior ag or industrial rights through SEO e-Permit and a Wyoming water-rights attorney.

Sources: BLM Dry Creek Trona approval; Reclamation Fontenelle operations page; `research/raw/sweetwater/2026-06-13-stage3-water-economics.md`.

### Land

The land target is not a listing search. The synced repo identifies the actual land path as a negotiated purchase from Sweetwater Royalties / New URC.

The 2026 URC transaction confirms Sweetwater Royalties owns one of the largest private land positions in the U.S., including about 850,000 fee surface acres and about 4.5 million fee mineral acres across the land-grant estate. The buyer's stated thesis is long-term mineral and uranium optionality, not surface liquidation. That makes a 5,000-10,000 acre entry block plausible but opportunistic: it likely has to be a negotiated one-off, timed after the ownership/decision-rights settle.

Immediate action: do not assume a public listing exists. Engage deal counsel / asset-side contacts to test surface-sale appetite after the expected URC close. Use the parcel search to define the requested block, not to find the seller.

Sources: URC / Sweetwater Royalties transaction release; `research/raw/sweetwater/2026-06-13-stage3-land-labor.md`.

### County Posture

County posture remains favorable and is not the binding constraint. The important correction is that "friendly county" does not eliminate the real veto stack:

- surface landholder;
- BLM / Rock Springs RMP if federal exchange or federal right-of-way enters the path;
- sage-grouse / ACEC overlays;
- water firmness;
- PacifiCorp / PSC at build-out scale.

Immediate action: keep the county development-agreement path alive, but do not ask the county to bless a speculative site. Tie the DA term sheet to a real surface block, water path, and grid/power approach.

Sources: `results/wy-sweetwater/county-sweetwater.md`; `research/raw/sweetwater/2026-06-13-federal-layer-pull.md`.

## Parcel Search Specification

The next search should not ask "is Sweetwater good?" It should ask whether there is a specific block with these attributes:

| Gate | Pass Condition | Fail Condition |
|---|---|---|
| Basin | Green River side / within feasible conveyance to Green River or JPWB node | Great Divide Basin interior dependent on saline groundwater/desal |
| Seller | Sweetwater Royalties / New URC fee surface block, negotiable without BLM exchange | scattered checkerboard requiring multi-year FLPMA exchange |
| Size | 5,000-10,000 acre entry block, expandable by option | no coherent entry block or only mineral-encumbered/unavailable sections |
| Federal layer | outside worst ACEC/core-area/sage-grouse density constraints | high-conflict RMP / core-area block where layout is capped or exchange is required |
| Water | firming path via Fontenelle contract or senior-right acquisition | only new junior right with no drought-firm backup |
| Power | entry below 25 MW or studied 25-199 MW path | build-out promise at or above 200 MW before study / generation plan |
| County | DA + vested plan politically feasible | county posture turns conditional or hostile after site visibility |

## Highest-Value Missing Facts

1. Exact candidate polygon or section list.
2. GIS overlay: Sweetwater Royalties fee surface x BLM surface x Rock Springs RMP/ACEC x sage-grouse core x Green River conveyance distance.
3. Confirmation that the URC/Sweetwater transaction closed and who controls surface-sale decisions.
4. Fontenelle M&I storage availability, pricing, and contract path.
5. SEO e-Permit search for senior acquirable rights in the relevant sub-basin.
6. PacifiCorp system-impact study scope and whether entry load can stay under 25 MW.

## Revised Read

Sweetwater remains the only pilot county worth site-level spend, but only under a tighter thesis:

> Enter small on a Green River corridor block bought from Sweetwater Royalties / New URC, firm water through storage or senior-right acquisition, keep BLM off the critical path, and defer any 200 MW build-out commitment until the power strategy is studied.

The first real "site search" deliverable should therefore be a parcel package map, not another county memo.
