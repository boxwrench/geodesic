# Depth 1 Search Packet

Date: 2026-06-20  
Status: draft; browser/desk search packet  
Scope: next-depth work after `site-focused-search-all-sites-2026-06-20.md`  
Rule: note a kill finding, but do not stop at it. Continue until the narrowed rescue case is actually tested.

## Purpose

This packet turns the next search depth into executable pulls. It is not another narrative report. The goal is to produce a source-backed AOI packet for each site:

1. candidate parcel or parcel cluster;
2. ownership and large-tract evidence;
3. water source / right / service path;
4. flood, wetland, protected-land, and federal overlay;
5. power / generation / interconnection implications;
6. county posture evidence from observed decisions.

The output should be a short source bundle for each county plus a one-page go/no-go update. If a site is killed at one gate, keep collecting the other gates until the project learns whether the kill is narrow, scale-dependent, or structural.

## Common Pulls For Every Site

### Physical Overlay Pull

Use the same overlay stack so the sites stay comparable:

- FEMA NFHL effective flood hazard zones.
- USFWS NWI wetland polygons.
- USGS 3DEP / state LiDAR where hydrology matters.
- PAD-US / state conservation layers.
- BLM or federal surface where western checkerboard matters.
- HIFLD transmission and substations.
- County parcel or assessor layer where available.
- Road / rail / municipal boundary.

Record the date, portal, exact search geography, and whether the layer was downloaded, screenshotted, or only visually inspected.

### County Posture Pull

For each county, pull at least 24 months where records exist:

- board / commission agendas and minutes;
- planning or zoning commission records, if the body exists;
- large subdivision, industrial, energy, mining, utility, CAFO, or resource-project decisions;
- moratoria, zoning rewrites, unusual approval conditions, or litigation;
- recurring opposition speakers or organizations.

The evidence to capture is not "friendly/unfriendly." It is observed behavior: approvals, denials, delays, conditions, and who can make noise.

### Large-Tract Pull

Do not use public listings alone. For each county:

- parcel/assessor search for single owners or related entities above 5,000 acres;
- ranch/timber broker pages and listing aggregators;
- state/federal/tribal/conservation ownership exclusions;
- advertised water rights or irrigation rights;
- mineral severance and federal adjacency where visible.

State the bounded search method before making any "no large site" claim.

## Sweetwater WY

### Decision To Resolve

Can we define a real, optionable 5,000-10,000 acre Green River / I-80 corridor block, preferably on Sweetwater Royalties / New URC fee surface, that avoids the worst federal/sage-grouse constraints and has a firm water path?

### Starting Thesis

Sweetwater remains the strongest candidate, but only if the search moves from county-level to parcel-package diligence. The target is not "somewhere in Sweetwater County." It is a corridor block that keeps surface water reachable, keeps BLM off the critical path where possible, and defers any 200 MW commitment until power is studied.

### Browser / Desk Pulls

| Pull | Source path | Question answered | Pass signal | Fail signal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Candidate AOI sketch | County parcel/assessor, land-grant ownership records, BLM surface, public land maps | Is there a coherent 5k-10k acre block on or near Sweetwater Royalties / New URC fee surface? | Single or controlled seller block; expandable option; Green River side | Scattered checkerboard that forces FLPMA exchange or multi-owner assembly |
| Federal overlay | BLM Rock Springs RMP maps, ACEC, sage-grouse core / priority habitat | Does the block avoid the worst federal veto overlays? | Layout avoids highest-conflict ACEC/core areas | Block is capped by RMP/sage-grouse constraints or requires federal exchange |
| Water-right search | WY SEO e-Permit, Green River basin water-plan files, Pacific Soda / Dry Creek right details | Is there a senior or firmable water path beyond a new junior right? | Fontenelle storage path or acquirable senior right identified | Only new junior right with no drought-firm backup |
| Fontenelle storage | Reclamation Fontenelle operations, WWDC / Wyoming water-development files | Is storage water plausibly contractable? | Uncommitted M&I storage exists or contact path is clear | No available storage or unresolved post-2026 risk dominates |
| JPWB headroom | JPWB budget/capital plan and direct capacity docs | Can entry ride existing treated-water headroom? | Entry contract possible, build-out brings own firm supply | Existing system unwilling or no spare capacity |
| Power threshold | Rocky Mountain Power large-service process, OASIS/queue where visible | Can entry avoid the large-load cliff? | Entry below 25 MW or 25-199 MW study path | >200 MW promise before study/special-contract plan |
| County posture | Sweetwater commission/planning records and development-agreement precedents | Is the county still helpful once a real site is visible? | DA/vesting path can be tied to actual parcel | County support is vague or conditional around site-specific impacts |

### First AOI Bias

Start with Green River / Rock Springs / I-80 corridor blocks before Great Divide Basin interior blocks. A corridor site keeps surface water and JPWB/Green River conveyance in play. A Great Divide Basin interior site likely pushes toward saline groundwater/desalination, which should be treated as a fallback, not the base case.

### Human / Call List After Desk Pull

- Sweetwater Royalties / New URC surface-sale decision maker or deal counsel.
- JPWB for actual uncommitted capacity and wholesale/industrial terms.
- Reclamation Upper Colorado Region and WWDC for Fontenelle M&I storage.
- Wyoming water-rights attorney for e-Permit senior-right screen.
- PacifiCorp / Rocky Mountain Power for large-load study intake.
- Sweetwater County attorney / planner / economic-development contact for DA timing.

### Stop / Continue Logic

If no coherent optionable block exists, Sweetwater loses the current site-level thesis. Continue water and power pulls anyway, because they become reusable state/county method improvements.

## Jones NC

### Decision To Resolve

Is there any private, contiguous, dry or mostly dry, flood-safe, water-serviceable block of at least 5,000 acres? If yes, can it scale toward 10k-15k acres without hitting a jurisdictional-wetland or regional-water wall?

### Starting Thesis

Jones is no longer cleanly killed by the old wetlands framing. Post-Sackett and NC SB 582 make isolated wetlands less legally fatal than the first screen implied. The physical problem remains: flood, hydrology, buildable upland, and water service.

### Browser / Desk Pulls

| Pull | Source path | Question answered | Pass signal | Fail signal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Parcel / ownership search | Jones County GIS or tax parcel records; NC OneMap if county portal fails | Does any private 5k+ acre dry-upland block exist? | One or few private owners; upland core visible | Only wet, public, conservation, or heavily fragmented blocks |
| NWI overlay | USFWS Wetlands Mapper / NWI data | How much of the candidate block is mapped wetland? | Buildable upland or isolated wetland dominates | Surface-connected wetland/floodplain dominates |
| FEMA flood overlay | FEMA NFHL effective and preliminary layers | Is the block outside flood hazard and repeat-flood behavior? | Upland outside AE/VE and away from Trent/Pollocksville flood behavior | Floodplain or low-relief stormwater risk consumes buildable area |
| LiDAR / drainage | NC LiDAR / USGS 3DEP; SSURGO hydric soils | Does the land actually drain like buildable upland? | Elevation and soils support site planning | Microtopography shows wet, flat, hard-to-drain block |
| CCPCUA water path | NC DEQ CCPCUA pages, LWSPs, Jones County system, NRWASA | Can entry and build-out be served without capped deep groundwater? | Entry local/Castle Hayne or NRWASA; build-out surface-water path | No wholesale/surface-water path; groundwater only |
| NRWASA / Neuse path | NRWASA, Kinston, NC DWR withdrawal/IBT materials | Can a 24-mile surface-water solution be real? | Capacity/willingness and no IBT trigger | Contract unwillingness, IBT, wetland pipeline corridor, or cost wall |
| County posture | Jones commission/planning records, Google Drive minutes/videos | Do recent decisions support large private development? | Low local veto plus observed approvals/no moratoria | Hidden opposition or capacity failure in actual records |

### First AOI Bias

Search away from Trent River / Pollocksville flood behavior and away from Croatan/Hofmann/conservation-heavy blocks. The first pass should be a negative-space search: find the private land that remains after removing federal/state/conservation land, NWI wetland, NFHL flood hazard, and obvious low-elevation drainage constraints.

### Human / Call List After Desk Pull

- NC DWR CCPCUA staff for grantability of any new allocation in Jones.
- NRWASA for surplus, member-area limits, wholesale appetite, and rate structure.
- Jones County manager / EDC / public works for water-system expansion plans.
- USACE Wilmington District for post-Sackett jurisdictional determination posture in Jones/pocosin settings.
- Wetland consultant for preliminary desktop JD once candidate AOI exists.

### Stop / Continue Logic

If no dry private 5k-acre block exists, Jones fails even at the scaled-down reopen case. Continue water-service and county-posture pulls anyway, because the water lesson generalizes to other NC coastal-plain counties.

## Fall River SD

### Decision To Resolve

Is there a small-scale rescue case where a 5k-15k acre ranch block comes with attached or acquirable water rights sufficient for a low-thousands entry project, even though 50k build-out water remains killed?

### Starting Thesis

The 50k build-out water kill stands. The useful remaining search is not "can Fall River host Geodesic build-out?" It is whether land plus attached/acquirable water supports a much smaller first project and whether that narrower case is worth preserving.

### Browser / Desk Pulls

| Pull | Source path | Question answered | Pass signal | Fail signal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ranch block search | Fall River assessor/parcel records, ranch brokers, Land.com/LandWatch-style listings, auction pages | Are there 5k-15k acre blocks near Edgemont/Hot Springs with plausible services? | Single-owner ranch block, road/power access, water indicators | Only fragmented, remote, or federally constrained blocks |
| Attached water rights | SD DANR water-rights database, deeds/listings, broker packets | Do ranches carry rights that can move to the project use? | Existing right with usable volume/source and transfer path | Stock/domestic only, non-transferable, too junior, or too small |
| New / amended right path | DANR Water Rights Program, public notices, Water Management Board records | Could a small municipal/industrial appropriation pass? | Low-thousands scale appears under regional opposition threshold | Any new appropriation triggers Powertech-style fight |
| Aquifer headroom | USGS SIR 2025-5067 subarea tables; well-completion records | Is southern Black Hills recharge less constrained than Rapid City-side signal? | Local subarea headroom exists | Madison/Inyan Kara/Minnelusa already at or above recharge |
| Surface / Angostura path | Reclamation Angostura Division, irrigation-district context | Can ag/storage water be acquired/converted? | Small conversion path plausible | Reclamation/district/NEPA blocks it |
| Self-built power siting | SD PUC siting, fuel/transmission proximity, water needs | Does project-owned generation help without worsening water? | Low-water generation strategy and feasible siting | Thermal water needs or cultural/visual opposition becomes new gate |
| County posture | Fall River commission/minutes; uranium, energy, CAFO, subdivision examples | Is no-zoning actually useful, or just low-capacity? | Recent large approvals with little process risk | Reactive politics or external opposition dominates |

### First AOI Bias

Start around actual ranch blocks, not abstract county geography. Record:

- acres;
- owner/seller;
- water rights or wells advertised;
- source aquifer / surface source if known;
- distance to Hot Springs, Edgemont, Angostura, highways, and transmission;
- federal/tribal/protected adjacency;
- karst/sinkhole risk around Hot Springs side.

### Human / Call List After Desk Pull

- SD DANR Water Rights Program / Water Management Board staff.
- South Dakota water-rights attorney for change-of-use and transfer constraints.
- Ranch brokers with Fall River / southern Black Hills inventory.
- Fall River County clerk / commission staff for recent large-project history.
- Hot Springs and Edgemont public works for current water-system headroom.

### Stop / Continue Logic

If no attached/acquirable water path exists for a low-thousands entry project, Fall River remains killed. Continue land and county-posture pulls because they correct the earlier implication that the county lacked large sites or local permissiveness.

## Depth 1 Output Format

Create one short file per site under `results/{site}/depth-1/`:

```text
source-log.md
parcel-candidates.md
water-path.md
physical-overlays.md
county-posture.md
decision-update.md
```

Each file should use this minimum evidence table:

| Claim | Source | Pull date | Evidence type | Confidence | What would change it |
|---|---|---:|---|---|---|

Confidence labels:

- `confirmed`: primary source or direct portal record retrieved.
- `probable`: strong secondary source or indirect primary evidence.
- `open`: located source path, but record not retrieved.
- `blocked`: portal, paywall, public-records request, or human call needed.
